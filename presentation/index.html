Slide 06 — The Engine

THE ANALYTICAL
ENGINE

🕸️
Community Detection
Groups criminals into clusters using weighted label propagation algorithm. Analyzes communication density and financial flows to identify who works together.
→ "Who operates in the same criminal cell?"
🌉
Bridge Detection
Identifies entities with high betweenness centrality — people who connect different criminal groups. These are often the most valuable intelligence targets.
→ "Who links separate criminal networks?"
⭐
Influence Scoring
Ranks every entity on a 0-100 attention score using weighted PageRank, degree centrality, anomaly detection, and cross-source evidence breadth.
→ "Who is the most analytically significant?"
🚨
Anomaly Detection
Statistical anomaly detection across four dimensions: communication bursts, transaction outliers, suspicious co-location clusters, and structural bridge entities.
→ "What activity deviates from normal patterns?"
